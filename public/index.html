What is SEOBoost?

SEOBoost is a free, AI-powered SEO optimization tool designed to help website owners, bloggers, and digital marketers improve their search engine rankings. Our tool analyzes your website for common SEO issues and provides actionable recommendations to boost your visibility on Google and other search engines.

With SEOBoost, you can quickly audit any webpage for critical SEO factors including title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, image alt text, Open Graph tags, canonical URLs, and content quality. Our intelligent analysis engine scans your HTML and provides a detailed score along with specific issues and fixes.

Key Features

  • • URL Analyzer: Get a comprehensive SEO audit of any webpage. Check title tags, meta descriptions, heading hierarchy, image optimization, social media tags, and technical SEO factors.
  • • Content Optimizer: Paste your content and receive AI-powered suggestions for improvement. Generate optimized meta tags, rewrite content for better engagement, and boost readability scores.
  • • Keyword Research: Discover high-performing keywords with difficulty ratings and search volume estimates. Find the best keywords to target for your niche.
  • • PageSpeed Test: Test your website loading speed and performance. Get scores for Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO with detailed metrics.
  • • Backlink Checker: Analyze backlinks pointing to your website. See total backlink count, referring domains, and source URLs.
  • • SERP Preview: Preview how your page appears in Google search results. Optimize title and description length for maximum click-through rate.
  • • Schema Generator: Generate structured data markup (JSON-LD) for Articles, Products, FAQs, and Local Businesses to enhance search result visibility.
  • • Readability Score: Check content readability with Flesch scoring, grade level analysis, word count, sentence statistics, and readability recommendations.

Why SEO Matters

Search Engine Optimization is essential for driving organic traffic to your website. A well-optimized site ranks higher on Google, attracts more visitors, and converts better. SEOBoost helps you identify and fix the most common SEO problems that hold your site back from reaching its full potential.

Whether you are a beginner blogger or an experienced webmaster, SEOBoost provides the insights you need to make data-driven decisions about your content strategy and technical SEO setup.
